# NXG Social / Content Contract

Platforms:
Facebook, Facebook Groups, Instagram, LinkedIn, Google Business, YouTube, TikTok, Pinterest, X.

Entities:
SocialAccount, SocialConnection, Community, Post, Campaign, PublishingManifest, PerformanceRecord.

Pipeline:
Canonical content → channel adaptation → asset resolution → approval → schedule/publish → external ID → metrics.

Facebook featured-service posts and group posts are represented as channel-specific post types, not separate business entities.

Never automatically post into a group unless the integration has explicit authorization and the configured workflow permits it.
