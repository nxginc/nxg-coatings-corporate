# Government / Grants Contract

The government/grants subsystem supports discovery, eligibility, evidence, compliance, proposal drafting, submission tracking, awards and reporting.

Every important assertion should have source/evidence metadata.

Do not fabricate:
- registrations
- certifications
- socioeconomic status
- past performance
- insurance/bonding
- financial information
- legal representations

Submission requires an authorized human approval event.

The proposal engine may consume approved project/takeoff/pricing records.
